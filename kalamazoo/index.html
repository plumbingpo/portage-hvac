Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Kalamazoo

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
