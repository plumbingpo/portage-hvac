Keeping Your Lansing HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a few miserable nights.
